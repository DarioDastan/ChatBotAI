About Izocrete

Izocrete is a revolutionary building material that combines the strength of concrete with the low density of polystyrene, creating a lightweight, highly insulating, and fire-resistant material.

Key Benefits

  • Superior Insulation: Eight times better than red bricks
  • Energy Efficiency: Reduces heating and cooling costs by up to 70%
  • Lightweight: Half the weight of traditional materials
  • Fire Resistant: Non-combustible for enhanced safety
  • Easy Installation: Large blocks reduce labor and material use

Applications

Izocrete blocks are ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. They're perfect for walls, partitions, floors, roofs, and façade cladding.

Thermal Performance

Thermal conductivity of 0.06 W/m·K means exceptional energy efficiency and comfort.

Cost Effective

Long-term savings through reduced energy costs and faster construction times.

Environmentally Friendly

Supports sustainable construction practices and reduces carbon footprint.
